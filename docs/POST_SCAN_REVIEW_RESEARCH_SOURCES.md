# Post-Scan Review Research Sources

Reviewed on 30 October 2026. These official product sources support the workflow patterns described in the approved *Lead71 Post-Scan Review Form: Research and Proposed Recommendation* PDF. They support mobile capture, custom lead qualification, and conditional properties; they do not define Aventure's dropdown taxonomy or authorize marketing consent.

| Official source | Relevant support | Accessed |
| --- | --- | --- |
| [Cvent LeadCapture](https://www.cvent.com/en/event-marketing-management/lead-capture) | Onsite lead capture, qualification questions, qualification, prioritization and follow-up. | 30 October 2026 |
| [HubSpot: Create and manage records in the mobile app](https://knowledge.hubspot.com/records/work-with-records-in-the-hubspot-mobile-app) | Mobile contact creation, business-card scanning, editable required properties and save flow. | 30 October 2026 |
| [HubSpot: Conditional logic for enumeration properties](https://knowledge.hubspot.com/properties/set-up-conditional-logic-for-enumeration-properties) | Show or require dependent properties based on prior selections; the page states this works in the HubSpot mobile app. | 30 October 2026 |
| [fielddrive Leads](https://www.fielddrive.com/solution/lead-retrieval) | Event lead qualification, optional notes and qualifiers, product interest, priority, and follow-up context. | 30 October 2026 |

The links were opened and checked on the access date above. Cvent describes custom qualification and follow-up; HubSpot documents mobile record creation and conditional fields; fielddrive describes optional lead qualifiers, notes, interests and follow-up context. Those examples are design evidence, not Aventure-approved option lists. The application therefore uses its existing Contact Type values and leaves Company Type and sales classifications as editable text pending Aventure's agreed values.
